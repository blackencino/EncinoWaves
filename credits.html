Encino Waves

Interactive Ocean Synthesis
Christopher Jon Horvath

This browser edition brings the School live viewer to the web: a real-time spectral ocean with wind speed, fetch, depth, directional spreading, persistent whitecaps, and environment lighting. The authored seas are illustrative presentation settings, not measured NOAA sea data.

The ocean

The browser simulation ports the School branch’s TMA spectrum, Hasselmann directional spreading, finite-depth capillary dispersion, seeded wave components, and horizontal displacement. Lower wave and display resolutions make it practical on laptops and compatible mobile browsers. The native viewer retains finer surface and foam detail.

Browser requirements

WebGL 2 with floating-point render targets. Try Light quality on a phone or a slower laptop. Basic pointer and touchscreen dragging are available; the controls remain based on the laptop viewer. This edition has no recording or movie export, no account, and no server-side processing.
