Source: DS360_Autumn_Pack_01_03a_Ref.hdr (4000 × 2000).
Derived assets: school-sky.png, school-reflection.png, sky.json lighting data.
The sky is resized to 2048 × 1024 and reprojected with the same 10-degree
horizon trim used by the native School viewer. GGX reflection convolution
uses 128 importance samples, matching the native filtering method.

PNG RGB stores sqrt(linear HDR radiance / the scale in sky.json), with
ordinary linear-light mipmaps for the sky and GGX roughness mipmaps for
reflections, arranged as vertical atlases. They are data textures, not
ordinary display images. Source HDRs are not included in this web bundle.

Rebuild using tools/build_web_sky.py and a separately supplied licensed source.
